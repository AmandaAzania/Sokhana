03 / OUR FEATURED INITIATIVE

Our human-centred AI learning initiative.

The proposed Sokhana Human-Centred AI Learning Initiative explores more inclusive ways of learning through education and research.

The proposed 18-month educational and research programme is planned for Hector Peterson Secondary School and focuses on learners experiencing barriers to learning, including specific learning difficulties such as dyslexia, dysgraphia and dyscalculia. Final participant numbers are being confirmed.

Planned activities include creative learning, coding, computational thinking, digital literacy, mentoring and appropriately used AI-assisted learning. Mixed-methods research will examine learner engagement, confidence, transferable skills and educational outcomes, with teacher and parent feedback informing improvements.

The initiative is in planning. Learner participation is voluntary and subject to school coordination, parent/guardian consent, appropriate learner assent, safeguarding, research and data-protection procedures, and funding. Media permissions will be requested separately. Academic improvements are not guaranteed.
